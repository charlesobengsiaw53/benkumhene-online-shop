Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions.

1. How do I place an order?

Browse our products, click Add to Cart, proceed to the checkout page, enter your shipping details, and place your order.

2. Which payment methods do you accept?

We plan to support Credit/Debit Cards, PayPal, Mobile Money, and Cash on Delivery (where available).

3. Can I return a product?

Yes. Products can be returned according to our return policy. Returned items should be unused and in their original condition.

4. How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on your location. Most orders are delivered within 2–7 business days.

5. How can I contact customer support?

You can visit the Contact page and send us a message, or contact us by phone or email during business hours.

6. Do I need an account to shop?

You can browse products without an account, but creating an account allows you to track orders, save items to your wishlist, and enjoy a faster checkout experience.

7. Is my personal information secure?

Yes. We are committed to protecting your personal information and using secure practices when handling customer data.
